# UX/UI audit against the arcade brief (October 5–6, 2026)

Spec: [docs/portfolio-ux-ui-rules.md](../portfolio-ux-ui-rules.md). Local work only: nothing committed or deployed.

Method: three read-only code reviews (desktop hall, phone exhibition, reading/content surfaces), then every
finding checked live before it was called a defect. Browsers: headless Chromium (D3D11, this PC's RTX), Firefox for
the loader links; dev server `localhost:4321`, local production preview `127.0.0.1:4331` (English routes exist only in
a build), and the live site for the loader links. Phones and tablets are Chromium emulation, not devices; no screen
reader output was heard (accessible names were read from the accessibility tree).

## Fixed and verified

**P0**
- *A typed Contact message was lost.* With focus on the send button or the privacy link, Esc/←/Home/R left
  `/contact/`, and so did a click into the room beside the panel; Back found the form empty. Now a form owns its keys,
  the room click leaves a started message alone (`form[data-dirty]`), the draft is kept in `sessionStorage` for the
  tab and cleared only after the service confirms, and while sending the fields are read-only and the button
  `aria-disabled` (a disabled button dropped focus to the page). `ContactForm.tsx`, `Hall.tsx`, `global.css`.
  Check: 11/11 (stray keys, room click, deliberate exit keeps the draft, slow send keeps focus, failure keeps text,
  confirmed send clears the draft); the network was intercepted, no message was sent.

**P1**
- *Hall keys hijacked reading.* Home/End/←/→/R opened other pages from a project, About or Contact panel, and on phone
  pages (also desktop at 400 % zoom). Phone pages now opt out entirely; in a panel the keys scroll it and only Esc
  leaves; letters (a/d/r) act only while the hall itself has focus; the screenshot digits 1–9 only inside the strip or
  close-up (WCAG 2.1.4). `Hall.tsx`, `CaseCaptures.tsx`.
- *Back reopened a closed project.* Closing (Esc, back link, room click) a panel opened from the hall now goes back to
  the hall's own history entry (`history.state.index`), so Back afterwards leaves the hall instead of reopening the
  project; a direct landing, a language switch in between and phone pages keep the old push. Focus returns to the
  console's open key. `Hall.tsx`, `focus-after-nav.ts`. Check: button/Esc/room/walk-then-Esc all PASS on dev; the
  language-switch case needs a build (English routes).
- *Console arrows dropped focus at the ends*, and the next Enter opened Contact: they are `aria-disabled` now and keep
  focus. `Hall.tsx`, `dock-hardware.ts`, `dock-hardware.css`, `arcade-interface.css`.
- *Accessible names did not contain the printed key words* (WCAG 2.5.3): "Öffnen: …", "Zurück: vorheriger Automat",
  "Weiter: nächster Automat"; phone cabinet links now read "<Projekt>: Antippen · Screens erkunden"; the language
  switch "DE / EN – Switch to English" (`lang` set); combined DE/EN labels split by language through `en-routes.mjs`.
- *"Alle Projekte" left work out.* Phone links went to `/work/` with the "Ausgewählt" filter (7 of 20); they now open
  `?filter=all`. The desktop directory gained a last row to the archive filter (its three projects have no station),
  labelled with the existing "Archiv / Archive".
- *Phone state.* The station crosses 900 px both ways (Hookline → hall station 11, Berry → exhibit), "Die Projekte"
  no longer leaves `#exhibit-…` in the address (it disabled the return-position restore), a tap is acknowledged at once
  and a late viewer module no longer opens over a page the visitor moved to, and a pinch-zoomed screenshot pans with one
  finger (`html.is-zoomed`). `MobileArcade.astro`, `mobile-arcade.css`, `gallery-lightbox.css`, `focus-clear.ts`.
- *Focus under the fixed header* (WCAG 2.4.11): Shift+Tab parked 15/53 (home), 27/46 (project), 10/49 (About) focus
  stops under the header or station selector at 390 px; now 0. `focus-clear.ts` moves only focused elements below
  whatever covers them; anchor scroll margins are untouched.
- *Breakout*: a held paddle key's repeats reached the hall and ended the round; *the directory* stayed open and modal
  after browser Back; *a stalled console download* left the console hidden and then failed the whole hall at its 30 s
  watchdog. The hall now stops waiting for the console after 15 s visible time and the console falls back to the DOM
  controls at 20 s (check: GLB never answered → hall lit at 22 s, DOM console usable).
- *Close-up caption over light screenshots* (1.1:1 at the first line over white): near-opaque band under the lines,
  12 px (median backdrop luminance under the text 116 → 23 on Snapsize's light theme).
- *Focus in the screenshot strip was invisible* (same style as the selection): own 2 px ice ring.
- *Header on a direct desktop project landing* stayed hidden until the room was ready while the panel was readable:
  shown at once there (the home reveal is unchanged).

**P2**
