## Cabinet construction

Use a shell/artwork pass and a distinct mechanical-control pass. Meshify may be explored for concept shells or decorative pieces if available; do not assume its output has usable topology or functional pivots. The detailed production controls should be constructed or corrected in Blender.

Keep joysticks, shafts, ball tops, dust covers, button caps, button housings, trackballs and switches as distinct controllable parts under stable named groups. Match the existing scene's control naming and pivots before export. A button cap travels inside its stationary housing; a joystick rotates about its physical base. Do not bake moving controls into a single decorative mesh.

Preserve existing action mappings, control bounds that include all animated descendants, and non-overlapping accessible hit areas. Generated icons are art-direction placeholders, not a specification for changing button actions. Pointer feedback must appear on the object that receives the click. Keyboard and touch alternatives remain available.

Prefer convincing bevels, glass thickness, satin painted metal, restrained edge wear, crisp artwork, inset screens and disciplined roughness. Screens must use actual project captures in implementation; generated mockup artwork is not a replacement for the project's real content. Cabinet designs should match each project's actual subject rather than inheriting incidental fantasy artwork from the first image.
